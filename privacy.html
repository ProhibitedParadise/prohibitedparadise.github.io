PRIVACY & DATA

FOLLOW ME / LOVE — Privacy information

Who handles your message?

Les Archives des Veilles operates the LOVE message form. For questions about your data, contact contact@lesarchivesdesveilles.fr.

What do we collect?

If you choose to send a message, we store the pseudonym you enter, your message, your publication preference, submission date, and moderation status in a Cloudflare D1 database. No account or email address is required to use the form. The application does not save your IP address in D1.

Why do we use it?

We use these details to receive and moderate messages, and, only if you explicitly opt in, to consider publishing your pseudonym and message in the Archives. A message is not published automatically. The publication checkbox is optional and unchecked by default.

Security and Cloudflare

Cloudflare hosts the message-processing service and provides Turnstile bot protection. It may process technical information, including IP-related data, for security purposes. Not storing an IP address in our D1 table does not mean Cloudflare processes no technical data.

How long do we keep messages?

We aim to keep private or pending messages for no longer than 90 days, and rejected messages for no longer than 30 days after moderation. Approved public messages may remain visible while the project exists, unless removed earlier upon request. Automatic deletion has not yet been implemented; these periods are planned limits, not an automated guarantee. The policy will be updated once the deletion process is active.

Your choices and rights

You can send a message without consenting to public display. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your message at contact@lesarchivesdesveilles.fr. Please provide enough information to identify the message without sending unnecessary personal details. We may need to verify a request before changing a message.

If verification does not work

You can contact us by email instead. Unlike the web form, email reveals your email address to the recipient. Email submissions are not automatically entered into the public archive.
